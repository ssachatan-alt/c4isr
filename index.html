🎯 CORE PROJECT AIM & MISSION
"To bring everything in one place"

C4ISRnova was envisioned and built by S SACHATAN to eliminate the fragmentation of tactical operations. Instead of switching between disconnected satellite portals, weather stations, Doppler radars, and unit tracking feeds, C4ISRnova integrates optical reconnaissance, global live meteorology across every country, and command-grade situational awareness into a single zero-dependency cockpit.
